E — Suggested content architecture

Cleaner section structure

To reduce repetition without removing useful information, consider this order:

  1. Hero: doctor name, specialty, location, one-line value proposition, verified credentials and primary appointment CTA.
  2. About the Doctor: biography, qualifications, clinical focus and approach to care.
  3. Conditions Treated: breast lump, fibroadenoma, breast pain, nipple discharge, benign breast conditions and breast cancer.
  4. Treatments & Procedures: BCS, mastectomy, sentinel lymph node biopsy, oncoplastic surgery, axillary lymph node surgery and reconstruction.
  5. Why This Practice: focused breast oncology, individualised planning, clear counselling and follow-up — avoid repeating procedure descriptions.
  6. Patient Education: article hub with unique topics and doctor review information.
  7. Care Journey: one clear step-by-step pathway from consultation to follow-up.
  8. Community & Survivorship: one short overview plus a gallery of distinct, consented activities.
  9. Clinical Governance & FAQs: verified clinical process, ethics, FAQs and medical disclaimer.
  10. Contact & Location: one authoritative address, phone, email, timings and appointment method.
Do not remove useful repeated navigation: repeating a Book Appointment button, phone number or clinic location in the header, mid-page and footer can be good UX. The audit flags repeated meaning in explanatory content, not every repeated conversion element.

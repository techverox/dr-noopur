Overall assessment: The two-page landing page communicates oncoplastic breast surgery, possible candidate profiles, FAQs, related procedures, hospital facilities, doctor credentials and appointment details. The key content issue is overlap: the definition and breast-reshaping message recur in the hero, main explanation, procedure card and footer. 01 · Priority fixes

Important checks before publishing

Avoid wording that implies surgery guarantees complete clearance or a specific outcome. Ask the surgeon to describe the surgical goal, margin assessment and role of pathology accurately.

This is a comparative clinical claim. The PDF does not provide a study citation, population, endpoint or limitations. Ask the surgeon to approve the exact statement and add an appropriate source if it is retained. Do not convert it into a universal guarantee.

Page 1 FAQ says radiation is essential after oncoplastic surgery to eradicate microscopic residual cells. Confirm the intended context with the surgeon and distinguish the role of radiotherapy after breast-conserving treatment from an absolute rule for every individual case.

Confirm participating insurers/TPAs, hospital coverage, policy conditions, pre-authorisation and exclusions. Avoid implying every patient or policy receives automatic 100% cashless approval.

Confirm whether all three components are available at the stated location and whether same-day ultrasound and biopsy can actually be offered in every advertised case. State availability accurately and avoid implying guaranteed same-day diagnosis.

The page mentions early-stage breast cancer, cosmetically sensitive tumour locations and large breasts benefiting from therapeutic mammoplasty to relieve neck/back strain. Confirm that each item is clinically appropriate for this page. Separate cancer-treatment suitability from general breast-reduction benefits if needed.

Page 2 lists diagnostic mammography, frozen-section pathology, dedicated oncology operating theatres, multidisciplinary tumour board consultation, oncoplastic and sentinel-node evaluation. Confirm current availability and approved descriptions with Marengo CIMS Hospital.

Check the phone number, email, full address, OPD schedule, Gujarat Medical Council registration details and all booking/WhatsApp links against official records.

The footer contains a medical advertising/communication guideline reference. Confirm the exact official document title, date and wording with a qualified regulatory reviewer before publication.

06 · Suggested cleaner page structure

Recommended landing-page flow

  1. Hero: Oncoplastic Breast Surgery in Ahmedabad, one concise patient-friendly summary and consultation CTA.
  2. Doctor / hospital trust card: verified designation, concise credentials and one hospital affiliation.
  3. What is oncoplastic breast surgery? one clear definition and the distinction from standard lumpectomy/reconstruction.
  4. Who may be suitable? concise, clinician-approved factors with an individual-assessment note.
  5. FAQ: specific, evidence-supported answers about recurrence/margins and radiotherapy.
  6. Related procedures: brief visual cards linking to lumpectomy, mastectomy and reconstruction information.
  7. Hospital facilities: verified facilities and services in one concise list.
  8. Appointment/contact: one primary consultation CTA, secondary WhatsApp and verified OPD/contact details.
  9. Footer: short doctor bio, navigation and approved disclaimer.
Important limitation: This audit reviews the visible text and layout in the supplied two-page PDF.

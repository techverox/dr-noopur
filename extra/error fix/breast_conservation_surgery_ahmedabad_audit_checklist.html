This is a consequential clinical comparison. Ask the breast surgeon to approve the exact wording, clinical context, patient groups and caveats. Avoid presenting it as a universal guarantee or personal treatment recommendation.

Confirm that the list is clinically accurate and sufficiently qualified. Eligibility is individual and should not be reduced to a simple checklist without clinician review.

Confirm the hospitals, insurers/TPAs, policy conditions and whether “100% cashless” can genuinely be guaranteed. If coverage depends on eligibility or approval, the page must say so.

Do not imply that surgery guarantees complete clearance or a specific outcome. Ask the surgeon to describe the intended surgical goal and pathology/margin assessment accurately.

Confirm these facilities and services are currently available at the named Marengo CIMS Hospital location and that the hospital approves the description.

Verify exact OPD days/hours, phone, email, hospital address, registration details and all WhatsApp/appointment links. Ensure repeated versions match.

Check the exact official guideline title, date and wording with a qualified regulatory reviewer. Do not publish an uncertain date or paraphrase as an official quotation.

06 · Suggested cleaner page structure

Recommended landing-page flow

  1. Hero: “Breast-Conserving Surgery (BCS) in Ahmedabad”, short plain-language explanation and consultation CTA.
  2. Doctor / hospital trust card: verified designation, concise credentials and one hospital affiliation.
  3. Who may be suitable: concise clinician-approved eligibility factors with an individual-assessment caveat.
  4. FAQ: balanced explanation of BCS and mastectomy, with evidence-supported wording.
  5. Related procedures: short visual cards and links to dedicated procedure information.
  6. Hospital facilities: verified services and location details.
  7. Appointment CTA: one clear primary consultation action and WhatsApp as a secondary contact option.
  8. Footer: short doctor bio, verified contact/OPD details, navigation and approved disclaimer.

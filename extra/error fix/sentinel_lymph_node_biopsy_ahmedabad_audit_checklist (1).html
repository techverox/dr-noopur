01 · Priority fixes

Important checks before publishing

Confirm the figure is specific to this hospital/surgeon, how it was calculated, the patient group and the source period. If it is not documented and approved, remove the percentage or describe the technique without a numerical success claim.

“Zero” is an absolute claim. SLNB may reduce the risk of arm/shoulder problems compared with more extensive axillary surgery, but it cannot guarantee no restriction or complications. Have the surgeon approve a qualified comparison.

Confirm this reflects the hospital's usual pathway and whether SLNB is performed with another breast operation. Present it as a typical estimate only if approved; discharge timing depends on the overall procedure and individual recovery.

The number of nodes identified/removed varies. Confirm the wording with the surgeon and avoid implying that every patient will have exactly one to three nodes removed.

Explain this as a treatment decision that depends on the patient's diagnosis, surgery, pathology and current clinical guidance. Avoid an unconditional promise that every negative result rules out further axillary treatment.

The PDF says a multidisciplinary tumour board decides whether limited axillary radiation or targeted completion lymph node dissection is required. Ask the breast surgeon/radiation oncologist to confirm the terms and decision pathway; avoid implying one fixed pathway for all positive results.

Have the surgeon approve the exact eligibility description and explain that suitability is individual. Do not present the FAQ sentence as a complete list of indications or exclusions.

Confirm participating insurers/TPAs, hospital coverage, policy conditions, pre-authorisation and exclusions. Avoid implying every patient or policy receives automatic 100% cashless approval.

Confirm whether all components are available at the stated location and whether same-day ultrasound and biopsy can actually be offered in every advertised case. State availability accurately; do not imply a guaranteed same-day diagnosis.

Confirm diagnostic mammography, frozen-section pathology, oncology operating theatres, tumour board, phone/email, full address, Gujarat Medical Council registration and fellowship wording with official hospital/doctor records.

Confirm the exact official document title, date and wording with a qualified regulatory reviewer before publication.

06 · Suggested cleaner page structure

Recommended landing-page flow

  1. Hero: SLNB in Ahmedabad, one-sentence overview, verified doctor/hospital details and one primary consultation CTA.
  2. Why SLNB may be recommended: explain targeted staging and potential to avoid more extensive axillary surgery, with appropriate clinical caveats.
  3. How the procedure works: tracer/dye, localisation, node removal and pathology assessment, only as confirmed by the treating team.
  4. Recovery: qualified information about arm movement, discharge and follow-up; include patient-specific advice.
  5. FAQ: eligibility and what a positive or negative result may mean, reviewed by the surgeon.
  6. Related procedures: brief cards linking to lumpectomy, mastectomy, reconstruction and oncoplastic surgery pages.
  7. Hospital and appointment: verified facilities, one contact/OPD block and clear booking options.
  8. Footer: concise doctor bio, navigation links and approved disclaimer.

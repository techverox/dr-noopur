Recommended landing-page flow

  1. Hero: local H1, concise patient-focused description, doctor/hospital identification and consultation CTA.
  2. Doctor / hospital trust panel: verified designation, key credentials and one accurate hospital affiliation statement.
  3. Surgical options: concise descriptions of BCS, mastectomy, oncoplastic surgery and other offered procedures.
  4. Decision-making FAQ: explain how options are selected; do not repeat the full surgical-options list.
  5. Related procedures: navigation links to dedicated procedure pages/sections.
  6. Ahmedabad service area: accurate nearby localities served, without implying unverified branch locations.
  7. Hospital facilities: verified infrastructure and MDT services, with approved claims.
  8. Credentials and contact: one concise credential summary, one authoritative contact/OPD block and a short disclaimer.

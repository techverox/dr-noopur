6 — Recommended content hierarchy

Cleaner structure for this page

  1. Hero: doctor’s name, approved specialty title and one-line positioning.
  2. Profile & credentials: concise biography, verified qualifications and registration details.
  3. Academic timeline: MBBS, MS, fellowship and advanced training in chronological order.
  4. Mentorship: only verified training/mentorship facts; avoid generic praise.
  5. Professional memberships: official association names, acronyms and membership status.
  6. Current clinical practice: current designation and hospital, with full address in the contact/footer section only.
  7. Appointment/contact: one consistent contact block and verified OPD schedule.
Clinical and regulatory verification: This is a content audit, not medical/legal certification. Credentials, fellowship title/acronym, registration number, hospital affiliation, membership grades, mentor claims, surgical claims and the NMC guideline citation must be checked against primary documents and approved by the doctor/hospital before publication.

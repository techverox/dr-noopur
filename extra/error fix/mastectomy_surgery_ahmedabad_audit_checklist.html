07 · Suggested cleaner page structure

Recommended landing-page flow

  1. Hero: Mastectomy Surgery in Ahmedabad, concise explanation and consultation CTA; remove guaranteed-clearance wording.
  2. Doctor / hospital trust panel: verified designation, concise credentials and one hospital affiliation.
  3. Types of mastectomy: surgeon-approved definitions of total/simple, modified radical, skin-sparing and nipple-sparing procedures.
  4. When mastectomy may be considered: a single clinically approved indication section.
  5. Recovery and drain care: one detailed section with qualified, typical time estimates.
  6. Reconstruction options: immediate, delayed and external prosthesis options, clearly distinguished.
  7. FAQ: concise answers that complement rather than repeat the sections above.
  8. Hospital and appointment: verified facilities, one contact/OPD block and a clear consultation CTA.
  9. Footer: short biography, links and verified disclaimer.

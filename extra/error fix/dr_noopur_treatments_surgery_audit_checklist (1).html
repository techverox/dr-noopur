01 · Priority fixes

Important checks before publishing

Ask the treating surgeon to approve the description and scope of each listed procedure. Do not imply every procedure is suitable for every patient.

Confirm the exact wording and current applicability with the doctor/hospital. Consider “guided by relevant clinical guidelines, where applicable” only if the doctor approves that wording.

This can sound like a guaranteed outcome. Have the surgeon replace it with precise wording describing the intended surgical goal and margin assessment, without promising a result.

The FAQ gives a specific “1 to 3 specific nodes” description and claims reduced risk of painful arm swelling (lymphedema). Confirm exact clinical wording, indications, limitations and patient-specific caveats.

Length of stay varies by patient, procedure, reconstruction, recovery and hospital protocol. Clearly label any durations as general estimates and have the hospital approve them.

Check phone number, appointment booking, Call Now, OPD hours, hospital name and address. Ensure each button works and opens the intended action.

The PDF uses breast/surgical imagery and doctor/patient-facing visuals. Confirm licences, patient consent where applicable, and that the images accurately represent the described procedure.

07 · Suggested content structure

Cleaner Treatments & Surgery page flow

  1. Hero: concise specialty positioning and consultation CTA.
  2. Featured procedure: one focused explanation of oncoplastic breast-conserving surgery, with careful clinical wording.
  3. Treatment portfolio: concise, non-duplicative cards for each procedure, each linking to a detailed section/page.
  4. Care pathway: the five distinct stages from assessment through postoperative follow-up.
  5. FAQs: patient questions that clarify differences, eligibility and practical expectations—not repeat card copy.
  6. Appointment CTA: invite patients to discuss options with the treating surgeon.
  7. Footer: compact practice summary, verified contact details and useful navigation.

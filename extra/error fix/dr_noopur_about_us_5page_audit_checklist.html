01 · Priority fixes

Issues to address before publishing

Check image crop, card height, text wrapping and mobile/desktop breakpoints. If the labels are embedded in an image, replace it with a properly exported asset or HTML text.

Match degree names, fellowship acronym, awarding institution, current designation and Gujarat Medical Council registration details to primary documents.

Confirm the hospital has approved each claim and that the described protocol/process is actually followed. Do not use verification badges as decoration or as a broad guarantee.

Verify the phone number, email, address, registration number and Mon–Sat timings with the clinic. The listed morning slot ends at 2 PM and the evening slot begins at 2 PM; confirm whether this is intentional.

Have the exact regulatory reference, guideline date and disclaimer reviewed by an appropriate qualified reviewer before publication.

09 · Suggested cleaner structure

Recommended About Us page flow

  1. Hero: one clear positioning statement, approved specialty title and appointment CTA.
  2. Meet Dr. Noopur Patel: concise biography, verified credentials and current designation.
  3. Clinical approach: three distinct principles or capabilities; do not repeat the hero paragraph.
  4. Mission, vision and values: short statements with different purposes.
  5. Professional journey: verified education, fellowship and career milestones.
  6. Multidisciplinary care: one full explanation with genuine, approved team/gallery images.
  7. Facilities: actual clinic/hospital spaces and equipment only.
  8. Clinical governance: counselling, privacy and second-opinion process, where verified.
  9. Appointment and footer: one consistent contact block, concise credentials and distinct navigation links.

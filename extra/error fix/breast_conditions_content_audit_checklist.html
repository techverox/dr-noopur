F — Recommended structure

How to make this page clearer

  1. Hero: one short introduction to breast conditions and a clear appointment action.
  2. Eight condition cards: concise definitions and links to unique condition detail pages.
  3. Breast anatomy: one focused explanation of breast tissue, ducts and lobules.
  4. Six warning signs: concise signs that warrant medical evaluation.
  5. Evaluation panel: short instruction on when to seek professional assessment.
  6. Educational guides: four distinct resources with no duplicated paragraphs.
  7. FAQs: answer common questions briefly and link to the relevant card or guide.
  8. Final CTA: one concise appointment prompt with verified contact details.
Clinical review required: The PDF contains medical statements, percentages, diagnostic descriptions and symptom advice. This audit flags copy overlap and items needing verification; it does not certify their medical accuracy. Do not change clinical meaning without Dr. Noopur Patel’s approval.
